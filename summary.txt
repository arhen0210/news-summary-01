President Trump declared the war with Iran "militarily over", shifting the conflict from bombing to economic pressure and a naval blockade, but Tehran says the Strait of Hormuz will stay closed until seven conditions are met. Secretary of State Marco Rubio, at a G7 foreign ministers' meeting in Paris, said sanctions and the blockade are working "so well" that there is "no need" for major combat operations against Iran.

Russia, Ukraine and the United States met in Abu Dhabi for their first trilateral talks since February, a roughly four-hour session that continued Wednesday. In Washington, House Republicans blocked impeachment charges against Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth 211-204 on a party-line vote, a tally Trump called an "exoneration". The GOP then chose Jim Jordan as House Republican leader, replacing Speaker Mike Johnson in January after sweeping midterm losses, while Senate Republicans reelected Senator John Thune of South Dakota as their leader. Bernie Sanders said he will not back Chuck Schumer for Senate majority leader, as did Senator Elissa Slotkin and several senators-elect.

In other news, Greta Thunberg arrived at COP31 in Turkey, her first climate summit since 2021, warning that without change food, clean water and functioning infrastructure "will become luxury". Two pile-ups on the Trakia motorway near Yambol in Bulgaria killed 11 people and injured 50, 13 of them seriously. Thousands of students and activists rallied across Indonesia over the government's response to forest and land fires, demanding a national disaster declaration and punishment for those responsible. And the US government received more than $12 billion in revenue from Venezuela's oil exports.
